## What is DDD?

**DDD: Don’t Dare Drink** is a party game where you learn about your friends while drinking.

Every card tells you what to do on the top, or how much to drink to get out of it, called a **punishment**, on the bottom.

Punishments are given in **sips**.

> A “sip” means: tip your head back, drink, then tip your head forward.  
> That is 1 sip. It is the honor system.

DDD is intended for **consenting adults of legal drinking age**. Play responsibly, look out for your friends, and never pressure anyone to drink or complete a card.

## Play online

The easiest way to play is through the official web app:

### [▶️ Play DDD here](https://spydar05.github.io/DDD/)

One person can host the decks while everyone else uses **My Cards** to keep track of their own current card, Specials, Curses, and Table Rules.

Your saved cards and host progress are stored only in your own browser. The site does not require an account.

## What’s in the game?

DDD includes cards across several categories:

| Category | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Truth** | Answer truthfully about your life |
| **Dare** | Do what the card says |
| **Jury** | The group judges your answer |
| **Duel** | 1 v 1, 1 v All, Team v Team, or All v All |
| **Reward** | Make others do a dare |
| **Rule** | Add a rule everyone must follow |
| **Special** | Save powerful cards for later |
| **Curse** | Attach negative effects to players |
| **Water** | Cards for playing near water |
| **Strip** | Optional stripping cards |

## How to start

1. Every player draws 1 card.
2. The oldest player starts.
3. Play continues clockwise in rounds.
4. On your turn:
   - Read your current card aloud.
   - Do what it says, or drink the punishment.
   - Discard the card face up.
   - Draw a new card.
   - Wait for your next turn.

A **round** is one full circle around the group. From your turn back to your turn again.

## 📜 Important rules

- Any player may choose to drink another player’s punishment to stop a card.
- Do not do cards that are not yours.
- Punishment sips cannot be given to the player who drew the card.
- No single punishment may be worse than a full drink.
- Nobody is ever forced to do a card.
- Every player can drink to get out of a card or leave the game.
- A player who left specifically to avoid a card can return by finishing their previous punishment.

> **Consent comes first.**  
> Nobody has to answer a question, complete a dare, drink, or keep playing.

## Creator notes

This game strongly encourages house rules.

Feel free to remove cards that do not fit your group, add your own cards, or adjust the experience however you like.

**Please do not drink and drive. You may die or kill someone.**

## AI / LLM usage

DDD was originally created in **October 2021**, more than a year before ChatGPT’s release.

It was made from thousands of Google searches, random ideas I had over the years, and suggestions from friends.

That said, the finished game does include a small number of LLM suggestions that I liked, mostly in the **Jury** category. The current website was also built using [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/).

If you prefer to avoid AI/LLM-created material, you can instead play by creating your own cards.
