How to Play & Rules

DDD: Don’t Dare Drink
Created by Alex Josephy

Don’t Dare Drink is a game where you learn about your friends while drinking. Every card tells you what to do on the top, or how much to drink to get out of it (called a ‘punishment’) on the bottom. Punishments are given as ‘sips’ of drinks.

What is a sip? Tip your head back, drink, tip your head forward. That’s 1 sip. It’s the honor system.

Starting Play

Every player draws 1 card. You must always have 1 card in your hand to prepare for your next turn. The oldest player starts, then gameplay continues clockwise in rounds.

1 ‘round’ is 1 full circle around the group; from your turn to your turn again.

Turns play like this:

  • Read your current card out loud to the group.
  • Do what it says on your card, or drink the punishment to get out of it.
  • Discard the card face up, revealing it to all players. Specials, Curses, & Rules are not immediately discarded.
  • Curse cards are always placed face up in front of you until discarded.
  • Special cards are kept secret for you to play at any time.
  • Rule cards are placed face up for all players to follow.
  • Draw a new card.
  • Wait for your next turn.

Core Rules

  • Any player can drink for any other player’s punishment, if they so choose, to stop a card.
  • Don’t do cards that aren’t yours. Example: if someone answers a truth card, you don’t also get to answer that truth for yourself.
  • Punishment sips cannot be given to the player who drew the card.
  • No single punishment may be worse than a full drink.
  • There is no forcing someone to do something.
  • Every player can always drink to get out of a card, or leave the game.
  • Any player who left the game specifically to avoid a card or punishment can come back by finishing their previous punishment.
Consent comes first. Nobody has to do a card, answer a question, or keep playing.

Category Rules

These explain what each deck is for and how its cards work.

Truth

Answer truthfully about your life. Players drink the punishment sips if they don’t have an answer.

Dare

Do what is on the card.

Duel

1 v 1, 1 v All, Team v Team, or All v All.

Reward

Make others do a dare.

Jury

The group judges your answer. Or you judge their answers.

Rule

Every player must follow the rules. A max of 3 in play at once. Every time you draw a new rule card, move the others to the right and discard the 4th to the right.

You must be ‘caught’ by another player for you to drink. This means you can get away with breaking the rules if no one notices.

Special

Positive modifier cards. These can be saved for later, played out of turn, and given to or traded to others for whatever deal you can make.

You do not have to show other players until you play it. If you draw a special card, draw again for your turn.

Curse

Negative cards that attach themselves to you when you draw them. Every time a player draws a curse card, they must put the curse face up in front of them on their turn.

Punishment sips count towards the discard sips. Example: if a curse requires 5 total sips to discard, and you have already drunk 3 sips in punishment of that curse, you only need to drink 2 more sips to discard that curse.

Water

Play near a body of water or pool.

Strip

All players are considered to have only 5 articles of clothing:

  • Upper body top layer (coat, jacket, sweater)
  • Upper body bottom layer (shirt)
  • Lower body top layer (pants, shorts, skirt)
  • Lower body bottom layer (underwear)
  • Everything else
